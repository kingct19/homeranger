# Brand guide

## Identity
Brand: Home Ranger Services. Category: residential heating, cooling, and indoor air quality.
Primary tagline: **Trusted comfort. Stronger homes.**
Service descriptor: **Heating | Cooling | Air Quality**
Voice: friendly, capable, clear, reassuring. Explain the problem and next step plainly. Use the illustrated technician as a welcoming guide.

## Color system
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ranger Navy | #0A2C5A | Headings, body copy, dark sections |
| Ranger Orange | #FF7A00 | CTA backgrounds, accent strokes, small highlights |
| Steel Gray | #6B7280 | Secondary copy on white |
| Storm Gray | #D1D5DB | Borders and neutral surfaces |
| Clean White | #FFFFFF | Main canvas and copy on navy |

These values come from the HVAC board. `#F6F8FB` is an added website surface neutral, not an original brand color. Use navy text on orange buttons. White text on orange and orange body text on white do not offer sufficient normal-text contrast. Use white on navy or navy on white for primary reading content.

## Typography
Reference boards name Monument / Monument Extended for display and Inter for body copy. Use your licensed Monument Extended files for major headings. The package does not bundle fonts or claim a license. CSS falls back to Arial Black for display and Arial for body text. Use real HTML text, not screenshots of typography. Suggested web sizing: desktop hero 56–72px, mobile hero 36–44px, section heading 32–44px, body 16–18px. Keep headings short and use uppercase selectively.

## Logo
Use the primary navy/orange logo on white or very light surfaces. Use the reversed white/orange logo on navy sections. Preserve the original aspect ratio. The included vector logos are adaptations; see ASSET-NOTES.md for lettering and fidelity details. Allow clear space of at least one quarter of the mark's height around the logo. Suggested minimum full-logo width: 180 CSS pixels; use the simplified favicon for small browser icons. Do not recolor, stretch, add bevels, or place over busy photos.

## Mascot
Use the technician once in the hero and sparingly elsewhere. Preserve face, uniform, cap, and orange accents. Avoid placing text across the character. Treat the character as an illustration, not a photograph of an actual employee. A decorative mascot can use empty alt text when nearby content provides its context.

## Layout and components
Use white backgrounds, large navy headings, orange CTA buttons, subtle gray borders, and generous spacing. Main content width: 1200px. Cards: 16px radius. Buttons: 8px radius, at least 48px high. Use a 4/8px spacing rhythm. Mobile service cards can stack or use two columns if labels fit. Use navy as the main background for an occasional CTA section.

## HVAC content
Suggested services: Heating, Cooling, Air Quality, Repairs, Installation, Maintenance. Suggested primary action: Request service. Secondary action: Explore services. Do not claim 24/7 response, licensing, insurance, financing, warranties, specific service areas, prices, or customer review scores until the business supplies verified details. Vehicle and uniform images on the reference boards are design concepts, not evidence of an operating fleet.
